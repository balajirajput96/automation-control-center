# Gemini Spark Daily Brief Prompt

Every day at 08:00 local time, review only the Google apps that I have explicitly connected: Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Slides. Produce a private briefing with the following headings: `urgent items`, `today's calendar`, `overdue follow-ups`, `automation blockers`, `documents requiring review`, and `top three priorities`.

Do not send email, create calendar events, modify files, share documents, spend money, or make bookings. When a potential action is identified, draft the proposed action and place it in a section called `approval needed`. Keep the briefing concise, cite the source app and item title where possible, and prioritize time-sensitive work.

This task should be configured as a daily time-based Gemini Spark schedule only after the Google account is signed in, Gemini Spark is available, and the selected apps have been intentionally enabled.
